Tax returns for NRIs, foreign assets and ESOPs

Returns with foreign income, foreign shares or ESOPs go wrong easily, and notices follow. We prepare ITR-2 with the foreign schedules done properly, and draft replies when a notice arrives.

Who we help

  • Residents holding foreign shares, RSUs or ESOPs from a foreign employer, or foreign bank accounts
  • Non-residents with rent, interest or capital gains in India, including property sales
  • Employees of startups exercising or selling ESOPs
  • Anyone who has received a notice, intimation or demand from the Income Tax Department

Where returns go wrong

  • Schedule FA left blank. A resident must report foreign assets, including unvested RSUs held in a foreign account, for the calendar year. Non-disclosure can attract a ₹10 lakh penalty under the Black Money Act.
  • Foreign tax credit lost. Credit for tax paid abroad needs Form 67 filed on or before the return.
  • ESOPs taxed twice or not at all. The perquisite is taxed on exercise; the gain on sale is taxed again from the exercise-date value.
  • NRI property sales. The buyer must deduct tax at source at non-resident rates. A lower deduction certificate obtained before the sale can avoid a large refund wait.

Dates for FY 2025-26

The 31 July 2026 date for non-audit individuals has passed. A belated or revised return for FY 2025-26 can still be filed until 31 December 2026, with a late fee for belated returns. After that, an updated return may be possible within 48 months, with additional tax.

Income earned from 1 April 2026 falls under the Income-tax Act, 2025. Returns for FY 2025-26 are still filed under the Income-tax Act, 1961.

Notice replies

We handle intimations and demands, defective-return notices, mismatch notices and scrutiny and reassessment proceedings. Each has a deadline stated in the notice; send it to us as soon as it arrives.

Questions we are asked

I am an NRI. Do I need to file a return in India?

If your income taxable in India exceeds the basic exemption limit, or you want to claim a refund of tax deducted, you should file. Capital gains on Indian property or shares usually make filing necessary.

My employer's RSUs are held with a US broker. Do I report them?

If you are a resident and ordinarily resident, yes, in Schedule FA, along with dividends and sales. We reconcile the broker statements for you.

  • ESOP and RSU perquisite and capital gains working

  • NRI property sale computation
  • Replies to income-tax notices
