Udyam (MSME) registration

Udyam registration gives your business MSME status: protection against delayed payments, easier bank credit and benefits in government tenders. The registration itself has no government fee; getting the classification right and using the benefits is where we help.

Classification, from 1 April 2025

CategoryInvestment in plant, machinery or equipmentAnnual turnover
MicroUp to ₹2.5 croreUp to ₹10 crore
SmallUp to ₹25 croreUp to ₹100 crore
MediumUp to ₹125 croreUp to ₹500 crore

An enterprise must be within both limits for a category; crossing either moves it up. Export turnover is left out when calculating turnover.

Why it is worth registering

  • Delayed payments. Buyers must pay micro and small suppliers within the agreed period, and never later than 45 days. Late payments carry compound interest at three times the RBI bank rate, and unpaid dues can be referred to the MSME Facilitation Council through MSME Samadhaan.
  • Leverage with buyers. Under income-tax rules, buyers who pay micro and small enterprises late cannot deduct the expense until they pay.
  • Credit. Collateral-free loans under the credit guarantee scheme, and priority-sector lending.
  • Tenders. Benefits such as exemption from earnest money deposits in government purchases.

What is needed

  • Aadhaar of the proprietor, managing partner or authorised director
  • PAN of the business, and GSTIN where GST applies. Investment and turnover are taken from income-tax and GST records.

Questions we are asked

Is Udyam registration mandatory?

No. It is voluntary, but you need it to claim MSME benefits, including protection against delayed payments.

We registered under the old limits. Do we need to register again?

No. Your classification is updated from income-tax and GST data. We check that it is correct and that your details are current.

What we handle

  • Classification check under the 2025 limits
  • Udyam registration and updates
  • Delayed-payment claims through MSME Samadhaan
  • MSME-1 returns for companies that buy from MSEs
