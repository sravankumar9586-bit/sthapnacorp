LLP annual filing: Form 11 and Form 8

Every LLP files two annual forms with the Registrar, whether or not it did any business. We prepare and file both, and remind you before the next due date.

LLP due dates

FormWhat it coversDue
Form 11Annual return: partners, their contributions and changes during the year30 May (within 60 days of the year end)
Form 8Statement of account and solvency, signed by the designated partners30 October (within 30 days after six months from the year end)
ITR-5Income tax return of the LLP31 October if a tax audit applies; otherwise 31 July, unless extended
DIR-3 KYC WebKYC of designated partnersOnce every three financial years, by 30 June

For FY 2025-26, Form 11 was due on 30 May 2026 and Form 8 is due on 30 October 2026.

Late fees

Each form late attracts an additional fee of ₹100 a day, with no upper limit. Two forms a year means ₹200 a day for an LLP that has fallen behind. Separate penalties can apply to the LLP and its designated partners under sections 34 and 35 of the LLP Act, 2008. Calculate your figure.

Does your LLP need an audit?

Accounts must be audited if the LLP's turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or its contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh in the year. Below those limits the designated partners can certify the accounts. A tax audit is a separate test under the Income-tax law.

What we need from you

  • Books of account or financial statements for the year, audited where required
  • Details of any change in partners or contributions
  • DSC of two designated partners

Questions we are asked

Our LLP did no business this year. Do we still file?

Yes. Form 8 and Form 11 are due every year until the LLP is closed. If it will stay inactive, closing it with Form 24 stops the yearly cost.

We missed several years of LLP filings. Can you help?

Yes. We file the pending years in order and give you the additional fee upfront. See overdue filings.

Can we close the LLP instead?

An LLP that has not carried on business for a year or more can apply to be struck off in Form 24. Pending returns are usually filed first. See closing a company or LLP.
