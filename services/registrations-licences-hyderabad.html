Business registrations and licences in Hyderabad

Which registrations a business needs depends on what it does, where it operates and how many people it employs. We work out the list for you and file each one.

The usual starting set

Industry-specific approvals

  • Pollution Control Board consents: CTE before you build, CTO before you start operating.
  • Fire NOC from Telangana Fire Services for buildings and premises that need one.
  • Factory registration under the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020, which replaced the Factories Act from 21 November 2025.
  • Drug licences for pharmacies, wholesalers and distributors.
  • Single-window approvals for new industrial units.

Once you hire people

  • EPFO and ESIC registration when the employee thresholds are met, now under the Code on Social Security, 2020.
  • Labour Welfare Fund contributions.
  • Establishment registration and contract labour licences under the new labour codes.

The four labour codes came into force on 21 November 2025, and states are still notifying their rules. Some registrations are in transition, so we check the current position in Telangana before filing.

Questions we are asked

Do I need both a GHMC trade licence and a shops registration?

They are different registrations. The GHMC trade licence covers trades listed under the GHMC Act; the shops and establishments registration is with the Labour Department. Many businesses need both, some only one. We check which apply to you.

Is GST registration compulsory for a new business?

Only above the turnover threshold, or if you supply goods between states, sell goods through e-commerce operators, or fall in another mandatory category. Many service businesses register voluntarily to work with larger clients.

Can you handle licences for a business outside Telangana?

Central registrations such as GST, Udyam, FSSAI, IEC and trademarks are filed online for any state. State and municipal licences differ from state to state; tell us your location and we will confirm what we can file.
