Trademark registration

Protect your brand name, logo or tagline across India. We search for conflicts, file in the right classes, and handle objections until the mark is registered.

How registration works

  1. Search. We check the Trade Marks Registry for identical and similar marks in your classes.
  2. Filing. Application in TM-A. You get an application number the same day and can use the ™ symbol.
  3. Examination. The Registry may issue an examination report with objections. The reply is due within one month.
  4. Publication. Once accepted, the mark is advertised in the Trade Marks Journal, and anyone can oppose it within four months.
  5. Registration. If there is no opposition, or it is decided in your favour, the mark is registered and you can use ®.

Choosing classes

Goods and services are divided into 45 classes, and protection applies only to the classes you file in. Choosing too narrowly leaves gaps competitors can use; choosing too broadly invites objections. We map your current and planned products to the right classes.

What we need

  • Applicant name and address: company, LLP, firm or individual
  • The mark: the word, or the logo as an image
  • Goods or services, and the date of first use if the mark is already in use
  • MSME or startup certificate, if any, which lowers the government fee
  • Signed authorisation for us to file (TM-48)

How long protection lasts

A registered trademark is valid for ten years from the application date and can be renewed for further ten-year terms indefinitely, in TM-R.

Questions we are asked

Can I use my brand while the application is pending?

Yes. Use ™ from the date of filing. Use ® only after registration; using it earlier is an offence.

How long does registration take?

It depends on objections and oppositions. A clean application can be registered within months; contested ones can take a few years.

Should I register the name or the logo?

The word mark protects the name in any style and is usually the priority. Register the logo separately if it is distinctive.
