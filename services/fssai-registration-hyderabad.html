FSSAI registration and food licence in Hyderabad

Every food business needs FSSAI registration or a licence before it starts, from home kitchens and cloud kitchens to restaurants, traders, manufacturers and importers. The limits changed on 1 April 2026.

Which one you need, from 1 April 2026

CategoryAnnual turnoverIssued by
Basic registrationUp to ₹1.5 croreFood safety registering authority
State licenceAbove ₹1.5 crore, up to ₹50 croreCommissioner of Food Safety, Telangana
Central licenceAbove ₹50 crore, and some businesses regardless of turnover, such as importers and exportersFSSAI

Until 31 March 2026 the limits were ₹12 lakh and ₹20 crore, so many small businesses now need only basic registration. Registrations and licences no longer expire; they stay valid unless suspended, cancelled or surrendered.

Documents

  • Photo ID and address proof of the proprietor, partners or directors
  • Proof of possession of the premises: ownership papers or rental agreement
  • List of food products or categories
  • For licences: layout plan, list of equipment, water analysis report where applicable, and a food safety management plan
  • Partnership deed or certificate of incorporation

After you get it

  • Display the certificate at the premises.
  • Print the 14-digit FSSAI number on labels, invoices and bills.
  • Apply for a modification before adding new product categories or premises.
  • File annual returns if your category requires them.

Questions we are asked

I run a home kitchen or cloud kitchen. Do I need FSSAI?

Yes. Most will need basic registration, as turnover up to ₹1.5 crore now qualifies. Food delivery platforms ask for it before listing you.

My state licence was issued under the old limits. What now?

It remains valid. If your turnover is now within the basic registration limit, we can tell you whether switching makes sense.
