Why the deadlines matter

MCA charges an additional fee of ₹100 a day for each late AOC-4 and MGT-7, with no upper limit. On top of that, the Registrar can levy penalties under sections 92 and 137 on the company and on officers in default.

If a company does not file its financial statements or annual returns for three continuous financial years, every director is disqualified under section 164(2) for five years, and the Registrar can strike the company off under section 248. Already behind? See how we clear backlogs.

How we work

  1. You send the audited financial statements and auditor's report, the shareholder list as on 31 March, and any changes in directors or shareholding during the year.
  2. We draft the board's report, AGM notice and minutes, and check the financial statements against the Schedule III format before filing.
  3. You review and sign. One director signs the forms with a DSC.
  4. We file AOC-4 and MGT-7/7A and send you the SRNs and challans the same day.

Questions we are asked

Can a small company file MGT-7A instead of MGT-7?

Yes. Small companies and one person companies file the shorter MGT-7A. We check whether your company meets the current small-company limits before filing.

Our company had no revenue. Do we still need an audit and these filings?

Yes. Every company must have its financial statements audited and file AOC-4 and MGT-7 or MGT-7A, whatever its turnover. Nil-activity companies may consider dormant status or a strike-off to reduce future costs.

We held the AGM late. What should we do now?

File AOC-4 and MGT-7 as soon as possible to stop the daily additional fee from growing. The late AGM itself is a separate default; we will tell you whether compounding is advisable.

Is director KYC included?

Updates to directors' contact details are included in our annual retainers. The three-yearly DIR-3 KYC Web is ₹499 per director on its own.
