    Questions founders ask

    Frequently asked questions

    What happens if AOC-4 or MGT-7 is filed late?

    MCA charges an additional fee of ₹100 per day for each form, with no upper limit. If financial statements or annual returns are not filed for three continuous financial years, every director is disqualified under section 164(2) of the Companies Act, 2013, and the Registrar can strike the company off.

    Is DIR-3 KYC still filed every year?

    No. From 31 March 2026 directors file DIR-3 KYC Web once every three financial years, by 30 June. A change in mobile number, email or residential address must still be reported within 30 days.

    Our company has had no business. Do we still need to file?

    Yes. AOC-4, MGT-7 or MGT-7A and the income tax return are due even with nil activity. If the company will stay inactive, dormant status or a strike-off may cost less over time.

    Who actually does the work I order?

    A verified professional assigned to your order: a Company Secretary, Chartered Accountant, Cost Accountant, advocate or specialist who handles that type of work. We check every professional's membership before they can take orders. Their name and qualification appear on your tracking page once they accept.

    Do you only work with companies in Hyderabad?

    No. MCA, FEMA and tax filings are made online, so we work with companies and LLPs registered with any Registrar of Companies in India.

    How do you charge for your services?

    We send a written quote for your specific requirement before any work starts. Government fees, such as MCA filing fees, stamp duty and trademark office fees, are charged at actuals and shown separately.

    How do I pay?

    You receive a written quote first. Pay by UPI, bank transfer, or card through a Razorpay link. We issue an invoice for every payment.
